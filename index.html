Straight Answers

Clear answers for venue owners

What does a commercial AV system cost?

It depends on how many screens and zones you need and how the space is laid out. We work to your budget and always quote a fixed turnkey price up front, so there are no surprise overruns.

How long does an install take? Will we lose business?

For a working venue we pull cable and build the rack during off-hours. A typical multi-TV bar is done in a few business days without interrupting service.

Can you use our existing TVs, speakers and wiring?

Often, yes. During the assessment we test what you have and reuse healthy displays, speakers and cabling where it makes sense, to save you money.

Do you support the system after install?

Yes — a workmanship warranty plus the manufacturers' commercial warranties, and optional support plans with remote monitoring and fast dispatch.

Do you work outside Winnipeg?

Yes. We're based in Winnipeg and take projects across Manitoba and the rest of Canada, including multi-location rollouts.
